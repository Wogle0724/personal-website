Apple · Smart Plans

June 2026

Problem

  • Plans are made in the group chat — and die there. The place a plan gets decided is the one place designed to scroll away, so by the weekend nobody remembers the time.
  • Saving it is a chore, and it's yours alone. You'd have to spot the right message out of a dozen, after the plan settled, and add it by hand — separately, for each person. So nobody does.
  • The little commitments vanish too. "I'll grab drinks," "can you bring the speaker?" — real promises made in passing, written down nowhere.

Proposed Solution — Smart Plans

  • You tap Smart Plans, and it drafts the plan for you. Apple Intelligence reads the whole thread on-device and pulls the day, time, and place the group landed on out of a dozen scattered messages — handing you one clean event, drafted and ready to send.
  • One tap, and it's only for you. A quiet "Add to your calendar?" card drops the event onto your Calendar and the prep onto your Reminders — privately, with no shared list to set up.
  • It catches commitments and keeps up. Promises become private reminders timed to the event, and when the plan moves — "push to 8?" — one tap updates it.
