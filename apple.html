Apple · Threads Intelligence

June 2026

Problem

  • The group chat blows up without you. Step away for an hour and you come back to a hundred new messages, a plan half-settled somewhere inside them, and no clue where to start.
  • Catching up is on you, and it's all manual. As you scroll through the extensive chat history, its easy to gloss over the most important parts.

Proposed Solution: Threads Intelligence

  • One tap, right in the thread. Threads Intelligence lives in the Messages + menu. Open it whenever the conversation has raced ahead of you, without ever leaving the chat.
  • It catches you up effortlessly. Apple Intelligence reads the whole thread on-device and distills it into a short summary.
  • The conversation becomes the plan. One more tap drops the event, complete with details, straight onto your calendar.
