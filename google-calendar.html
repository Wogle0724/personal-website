Google · Calendar Sync

June 2026

Problem

  • Most people don't have one life. They have several, split across several Google accounts. Each comes with its own calendar and its own tasks, and there's no native way to see them together.
  • Manual cross-posting. Keeping an event visible everywhere means inviting yourself, copying details by hand, and cleaning up duplicates later.
  • No single source of truth. To answer "am I free Thursday?" you check two or three calendars in two or three tabs — and even then, you are the one who has to notice the conflict.
  • Your time is one thing, but your calendars treat it as many — and the job of managing them falls entirely on you.

Proposed Solution — Master Schedule & AgendaAI

  • See everything in one view. Connect your Google accounts and view all of your calendars in one place. Easier to view, easier to manage.
  • An AI assistant. AgendaAI knows your schedule and speaks up before a mistake happens. You decide how much it sees; the more you grant, the more it can protect.
  • It reaches across accounts that can't see each other. A conflict hiding on your personal calendar gets caught while you're replying from your work
